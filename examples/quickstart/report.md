# Sales review

Written 2026-11-19 for the 2025 planning round.

## 1. Revenue

- East revenue grew 12.4% year over year in 2025.
- West revenue fell 6.6% over the same period.
- Total revenue in 2025 was $1.7M, of which East made up 64.0%.
- We expect total revenue to reach $2.0M next year.

## 2. Orders

- East took 455 orders in 2025, 45 more than the year before.
- Across both regions the average order was worth about $2,250.
- Returns stayed below 2% of orders.
